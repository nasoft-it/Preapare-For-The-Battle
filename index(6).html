Prepare for the Battle

When Teacher walks out, the war plans begin, Classmates all scramble — the battle's within! Paper balls pile like mountains of snow, Teams pick their sides and get ready to throw. Desks become castles, backpacks turn shields, Airplanes take off across ceiling and fields. One shouts a battle cry, standing so tall, Another ducks under as papers all fall! It's chaos extreme, the funniest fight, Till Teacher returns — then they sit extra tight.

by Abdullah Zia
